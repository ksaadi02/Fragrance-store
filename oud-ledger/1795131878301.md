# Oud Ledger

A lightweight Python and SQLite management application for tracking **stock**, **orders**, and **finances** in a Middle Eastern fragrance shop (supporting Attars/Oils, Eau de Parfum, Extrait de Parfum, Bakhoor, and multi-batch inventory).
